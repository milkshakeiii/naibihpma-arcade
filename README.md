# Naibihpma Arcade

Small Rust prototypes on a 400x300 VGA renderer, all following the pixel rules in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md):

- [`creaturecollector/`](creaturecollector/): Interstellar Forager, a foraging and tavern-cooking roguelike.
- [`starpond/`](starpond/): Star Pond, a level that lasts exactly one song.
- [`misenspace/`](misenspace/): Mise en Space, a live intergalactic kitchen whose chutes send dishes up to the same square of the dining room above. It is the redesign of [`chuteworks/`](chuteworks/), a two-board factory puzzle kept for its [design doc](chuteworks/docs/design.md) and puzzle-design research.

Monster Workshop started here and moved to its own repository, with its history, on 2026-11-07: [milkshakeiii/monsterworkshop](https://github.com/milkshakeiii/monsterworkshop). Its commits up to b7047f7 are still in this repository's log.
