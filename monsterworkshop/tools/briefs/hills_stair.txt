This is one screen of a top-down pixel-art village game: 25 tiles across and 16 down, each tile 16 pixels, shown four times larger. The thin dark lines are the tile grid. This screen is THE STONE STAIR, the way into the ORE HILLS, a province of rocky hills, copper mines and dark pine woods. Travellers arrive at the left edge on the low ground, up a mountain pass; the hills proper begin on the high ground at the top right, above a cliff, and the road climbs the cliff by a stair cut into the rock. Recompose the screen into a striking but welcoming gateway to the hills, the way a fine level designer and pixel artist would: think about composition, framing, clusters and open space, so it reads as a real place rather than a diagram.
