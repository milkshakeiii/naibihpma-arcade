This is one screen of a top-down pixel-art village game: 25 tiles across and 16 down, each tile 16 pixels, shown four times larger. The thin dark lines are the tile grid. This screen is COPPER CUT, in the ORE HILLS, a province of rocky hills, copper mines and dark pine woods. The hills' folk say more copper comes out of here than anywhere, and it does: this is a great open cut, terraced pits stepping down into the hillside, with copper seams along the terraces.
