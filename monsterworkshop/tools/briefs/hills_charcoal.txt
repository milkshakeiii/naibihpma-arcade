This is one screen of a top-down pixel-art village game: 25 tiles across and 16 down, each tile 16 pixels, shown four times larger. The thin dark lines are the tile grid. This screen is CHARCOAL WOOD, in the ORE HILLS, a province of rocky hills, copper mines and dark pine woods. It is a ridge of pines where charcoal burners smoulder logs under earth mounds, and a stonemason works granite into hammers and anvils. Above it, behind a cliff, are the hills' heights and the town of Verdigris, reached by THE STONE STAIR cut into the rock; on its right a cliff drops to the smelters of Bellows, and a vine carries charcoal straight over that edge. Recompose the screen into a smoky, working pine wood with a grand stair, the way a fine level designer and pixel artist would: think about composition, framing, clusters and open space, so it reads as a real place rather than a diagram.
