This is one screen of a top-down pixel-art village game: 25 tiles across and 16 down, each tile 16 pixels, shown four times larger. The thin dark lines are the tile grid. It is one quarter of PINEWATER, the weaving village where every player begins: cotton is picked in its fields, carded at a bench, spun into thread at a spinnery and woven into cloth at a loom house. The village has two such chains, one along its north road and one along its south street, so newcomers needn't crowd one. This is its north-east quarter, where the first chain's spinnery and loom house stand side by side on the north road, and the road leaves east through the hedge towards Tanglemere. The layout is settled: keep it exactly, and dress it.
