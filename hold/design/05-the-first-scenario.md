# Naibihpma Hold, document 05: the first scenario

Draft 1, 2026-11-27. The last of five design documents. It puts documents 01 to 04 on one map, from the first minute to Gorf, and ends with how the build would go.

**The scenario: "The Underbrush to the Hollow."**
- **The goal:** destroy Gorf's Hollow.
- **You lose** if the Hall falls *(demo)*.
- **Length:** 30 to 60 minutes (Henry, 2026-11-27), aiming at about 45.

## The map

The map is 100 by 45 squares: four screens across and three down, which fits the minimap at a pixel a square (document 04). West is home, and east is Gorf.

```
 W                                                                       E
 +------------------------------------------------------------------------+
 |  cliffs ^^^^^^^^^^^  (rock: caves go here)        reeds  ~~~   o far    |
 |   ^ Daot's cavern ^        ~ Star Pond              ~~bog~~     nest    |
 |  ~~~~~ stream ~~~~~                  o near nest   ~~~~~~~~      ::::::  |
 |   ~  [THE HALL]  ferns         o lone nest            o middle   :cursed |
 |  ~~~~  the Underbrush ~~~~~              ~~~ water ~~~   nest    : (H) : |
 |   ~      old growth         o near nest       sand ridge   o far  ::::::  |
 |  ~~~~  (ley stones)   ...    crows' roost     (dry)          nest  turtles|
 +------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

The sketch is not to scale. o marks a heron nest, (H) is Gorf's Hollow, and :: is cursed ground.

| Region | Where | What's there | Named after |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Underbrush** | the west third | The Hall on its pool, the stream, ferns and moss: damp ground all round the start | track 1 |
| **The cliffs** | the north-west edge | Rock, the only place caves can go: the Morel Cave, the Ironcap Cave, Daot's Grotto and the Olm's Cave | track 2, Daot |
| **Star Pond** | north of the Hall, 15 squares | A deep pond, the only place the Star Pond can be built | track 4 |
| **The old growth** | south of the Hall | Big trees and the ley stones. Damp in the shade, and where Gorf's Stones like to rise. | track 6, Ancient Magic |
| **The bog** | the middle third | Open water, reeds and the near and middle heron nests. Wet, and where leeches come. | |
| **The sand ridge** | across the middle, north to south | Dry ground between the bog and Gorf's side, two to five squares wide, with one gap of reeds | |
| **Gorf's side** | the east third | The far nests, then Gorf's Hollow on cursed ground, ringed by snapping turtles | track 5, Gorf |
| **The crows' roost** | the south edge, middle | A dead tree | |

**New placement rules:**
- Caves need rock (the cliffs).
- The Star Pond needs the deep pond.
- Everything else can go anywhere your buildings can reach.

These give the map a few decisions that matter: how far the caves are from the Hall, and what lies between.

**Water and dry ground decide the routes.**
- From the Hold to the near nests, the way is damp the whole way.
- To the middle nests it crosses some open ground.
- To reach the far nests and the Hollow, heroes must cross the sand ridge, and its one gap of reeds is where the herons wait.
- Puddles dug across the ridge (document 04) make new ways over.

## The start

The Hold starts small but standing, because heroes have to be there in the first minute (document 02). From the bare Hall, the first toad takes thirteen to sixteen minutes.

- **Buildings:** the Hall, the Burrow, the Twig Loft, the Chorus Log and Toad Hall.
- **Folk:**
  - eight newts, one of them Twen
  - two caecilians and two tree frogs
  - the Chorus Toad, at level 1
- **Heroes:** two toads with slingshots:
  - **Pip** is bold.
  - **Bufo** is timid *(the demo's two)*.
- **Stock:** 60 dew, 6 fungiwood, 3 cap ingots, 4 wooden shovels (document 02).
- **The time:** the first morning, just after dawn. The Hall's ferns are ripe, and the newts are already picking.

## The first minute

A guided start, as in the demo. It holds the world still while it waits for you and says one line at a time, above the panel. Nothing is written beside the game.

| Step | The line | What you do | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "YOU ARE THE ELDER. YOU CANNOT ORDER ANYONE." | Nothing; the view drifts from the Hall to the lone nest. | The Hold working by itself: newts picking and carrying |
| 2 | "A HERON NESTS CLOSE. OFFER DEW FOR IT." | Select the lone nest and press Reward, then Attack. | A reward set aside from the Hall |
| 3 | "PIP WILL GO. BUFO WANTS MORE." | Nothing; the candidates list shows Pip taking it and Bufo declining. | Heroes weigh rewards, and each names its price |
| 4 | "RAISE IT, IF BUFO IS WORTH IT." | Press More until Bufo takes it, or don't. | Raising a reward; company makes heroes braver |
| 5 | "THE CHORUS LOG CAN BUILD AN IRONCAP CAVE. IRON MEANS SPEARS." | The view moves to the Chorus Log and the cliffs. Select the Chorus Log, press Build, choose the Ironcap Cave, and place it on the rock. | Building from a building, and placing a cave on rock |
| 6 | "IT WAITS FOR 20 DEW AND 4 FUNGIWOOD. NEWTS WILL CARRY THEM." | Nothing; the Chorus Log's panel shows what it's waiting for, and newts set off from the Hall. | Things pass through hands before anything appears |

Then the world runs. The lesson should take about a minute, and the core, rewards and heroes deciding, is in the first half of it.

**After the first minute: hints, not lessons.** The first time each of these happens, one line appears and nothing pauses:

|---|---|

| The first raid is a minute away | "HERONS WILL COME FROM THE EAST." |

Each hint is shown once a game and can be turned off from the title screen.

## The road to Gorf

How a game is expected to go. The timings come from document 02's simulation, which has no enemies yet, and from document 03's draft schedule. So they're a target for tuning, not a result. Expect the real game to run slower. Nothing in the scenario waits on a time except the raids and the weather.

| Part | Minutes | What happens | What you're deciding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Morning** | 0 to 10 | The lone nest, perhaps one near nest. Caves on the cliffs. The first cap ingot (about 8). The first Sun Orb, perhaps by 8 (the simulation's stand-in; under document 03's odds it takes about six heron kills), and the Star Pond soon after. | Iron for slingshots or for the forge; which nest first |
| **Growing** | 10 to 20 | The middle nests start raiding. The first Gorf's Stone with the tenth building (about 10). The first leech pools. Spotted caps near the end of it. | Fight the stone early or build; how many caecilians |
| **The change** | 20 to 30 | Axolotls, Daot's Grotto and the Spawning Pool (about 22 to 25). The dry spell. Venom gear. The far nests start raiding. | Orbs for axolotls or toward the Olm; crossing the ridge |
| **The Olm** | 30 to 40 | Rain at about 30 opens the far side. The Olm wakes at about 36. Plates for the bullfrogs. | When to commit to the far nests |
| **The Hollow** | 40 on | Rewards on the turtles, then on the Hollow. Gorf comes out when it's breached. | How much dew to put on the last fight |

A game that goes well ends between 40 and 50 minutes. One that goes badly loses the Hall to a big raid in the 20s, or stalls at the turtles. A cautious Hold that never crosses the ridge doesn't lose, but its Gorf's Stones keep spreading.

## The music

The album plays from the first track, in order, and starts again after the sixth *(demo)*.
- **Nothing in the game waits on a track or changes with one,** and failing never cuts the music (the game-design rules).
- **It sits under the game.** At about 28 and a half minutes, the album plays a little over one and a half times in a 45-minute game.
- **The places are named after tracks** (see "The map"), but the music doesn't follow the view. Moving around the map never interrupts it.

Sound effects stay quiet and few, in the album's timbres: a croak, a splash, a heron's call. The Chorus Toad's dusk chorus is a sound effect, not the music (document 01).

## Building it

The documents are done when Henry has reviewed them. The demo's look, music and controls carry over. Its rules don't: they were the five-minute demo's own. The build would go in this order, each step checked before the next:

1. **The rules, headless.**
   - Port `sim/econ.py` into the game's Rust (`hold/src`), as the start of the real rules.
   - Add documents 03's enemies and combat.
   - Grow the bots: a scripted Elder, plus a "do nothing" Elder and a "rewards only" Elder, to find stalls and dominant strategies. Bots show whether one strategy always wins, but never whether it's fun.
2. **The map.**
   - The 100 by 45 map, with water, damp and dry ground, rock and the curse.
   - Scrolling, the minimap and unexplored ground.
3. **The screen.** Document 04's panels, buttons, keys and information screens.
4. **The art.**
   - Sprites for every kind at three sizes, the spots at four stages, and every building.
   - Day and night ramps.
   - All of it in the repository's pixel style (AGENTS.md).
5. **The scenario and the first minute.** The guided start above, then a fresh-player test by an agent that's never seen the documents, as the game-design skill requires.
6. **Henry's playtest,** with the in-game feedback keys.

**What would show up first.** Step 1 would show whether the rules make the stories in documents 01 and 03 by themselves, before any art is drawn. If they don't, that's the moment to change the rules, while change is cheap.

## Open choices

These are my defaults. Tell me if any should go another way.

1. **The Hold starts with five buildings and two toads,** not RockCollector's bare Denhome, so rewards are in the first minute.
2. **Caves need rock, and the Star Pond needs the deep pond.** Otherwise placement only decides distances.
3. **The map is fixed** for the first scenario, with randomness only in the creatures' rolls. A random map could come with later scenarios.
